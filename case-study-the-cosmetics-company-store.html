Why It Was Built & Project Scope

The brief was to make luxury feel accessible: a store that showcases prestige beauty brands at outlet pricing without looking discount or downmarket. The build leaned into an editorial, high-end visual style paired with a straightforward shopping flow, so the 'luxury at outlet prices' positioning came through in the design itself, not just the copy.
